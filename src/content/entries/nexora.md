**Nexora** is a game engine I'm writing from scratch in C++23. The long-term goal is a Nanite-like renderer.

The lessons are grouped into sections. The first one is [Nexora Core](/topic/core): files, time, logging, windows and events, the pieces everything else stands on. New sections will appear as the engine grows.
